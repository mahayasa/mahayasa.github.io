Bio

I am a postdoc at mathcomscicu, Chulalongkorn University working on synthetic data generation for health and genome data and a Visiting Researcher in the Faculty of Information Technology, University of Jyväskylä , Finland before that I received my PhD in Computer Science at AIDA Lab, College of Computing, Khon Kaen University. While pursuing my PhD I had a great opportunity on a research internship at Rebecca AI Lab, Feng-Chia University, Taiwan.

My research interests are synthetic data generation, tabular AI, and AI for health and biology. I am building a synthetic data platform for healthcare and biomedical data, and work as an AI/ML specialist and software developer.
